Crusade Salary

Every team's own roster, salary, items and fees, grouped by guild within that team — each team is its own independent battle with its own reward.

💎 How the loot gets split: show up and you split half the pot evenly with every other attendee. Bid gold and you split the other half, sized to your bid. Do both, and you take home both halves.

Only one? You just earn that half — example only, if the split is 50/50: Present but didn't bid = 50%, bid but wasn't Present = the other 50% (the real split is set per crusade in Attendance Share %).

🛡️ Won on Defense? The pot gets split 60/40 instead: 60% stays here and splits the usual way among this roster, while the other 40% pays out evenly to everyone who bid gold last crusade — a thank-you for fronting the gold, delivered on their next payday.

🚫 Lost the crusade? No loot at all — everyone's share drops to 0, no matter what's in the reward box.
